Overview

In partnership with Apple, Salesforce leveraged Agentforce to connect the Apple Messages for Business (AMB) channel with conversational AI capabilities.

Salesforce, Apple Partnership

NeuraFlash had the opportunity to partner with Salesforce to pitch a deal to Apple leveraging Salesforce's Agentforce technology within the AMB channel.

Designing Around Product Limitations

The initial approach required layers of research to ensure the design was feasible given the various product limitations at play.

Design Decisions

As a result of product limitations and platform constraints, I focused the design pitch around three key AI features:

CRM Driven Personalization

AI Generated List Picker

Enhanced Product Link

CRM Driven Personalization

To preserve a concise, text-based format, I made a deliberate trade-off between Apple's brevity guidelines and the demands of agentic response power.

AI Generated List Picker

I used AMB's existing list picker component to fill the gap, since Agentforce has no native equivalent.

Enhanced Product Link

I kept the Apple-native format and defaulted to the AMB standard, since Agentforce's link rendering was unpredictable and this ensured consistency.
